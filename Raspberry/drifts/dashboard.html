At start-up the LCD asks "Who's driving? Say your name." Record your name once and DRIFTS greets you in your own voice ("Hi," + your recording) when it recognises you. The recording (about 1 s) stays on the Pi, is never uploaded, and is listed on the privacy page. Delete it here at any time.
A profile holds your own eye, mouth and head measurements from the long calibration, saved on the Pi as Raspberry/profiles/<name>.json. The alert thresholds stay in the ESP32 firmware, so the car, this page and the LCD agree.
Settings

Calibration

1. Quick calibration

About 3 s. Look at the road with your eyes open: DRIFTS takes this as your road pose. Use it at the start of every drive.

--

2. Create profile

Long calibration · about 1 min

Nine poses, each held for 2 s: eyes closed, eyes open, mouth closed, mouth open, left, right, up, down, forward. Saves your own eye scale, yawn threshold, off-road limits and road pose.
