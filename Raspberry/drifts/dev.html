B – EYE ASPECT RATIO

EAR = (|P2 − P6| + |P3 − P5|) / (2 |P1 − P4|)

Two eyelid openings over the corner-to-corner width. It falls as the lids close; closed for more than 3 s fires the alert.

MAR = (|P2 − P8| + |P3 − P7| + |P4 − P6|) / (3 |P1 − P5|)

Three inner-lip openings over the inner mouth width. A yawn is MAR held above 0.5 for 1.5 s.

t = (iris − P1)·(P4 − P1) / |P4 − P1|² − ½

IV – HEAD · NAR

A – NOSE, ZOOMED (SUNGLASSES CHECK)

Looking for a face…

B – NOSE ASPECT RATIO

NAR = |P1 − P2| / |P3 − P4|

Bridge-to-tip length over nostril width. It rises as the head drops. With sunglasses a pitch drop counts as a nod only if NAR also rises 5 % above the driver’s neutral.
